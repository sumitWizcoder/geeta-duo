# Image Prompts – Geeta Duo

Image-generation prompts for every chapter and stage (lesson) in the app. Files go in `public/assets/` and are referenced by `concept.imageUrl` in `src/data/lessons.json`.

## How to use

1. Paste the **Global Style Prompt** at the start of every prompt (keeps all art consistent).
2. Append the **Lesson Prompt**.
3. Add the **Negative Prompt** if your tool supports it.
4. Export as PNG, 1024×1024 (square), transparent or soft-gradient background, then save under the listed filename.

### Global Style Prompt

> Child-friendly flat vector illustration with soft gradients and gentle rounded shapes, in the style of a modern educational mobile game. Warm, vibrant, spiritual-yet-playful palette (saffron, deep blue, teal, gold, lotus pink). Soft glow and subtle sparkles. Clean outlines, no text, centered composition, plenty of breathing room, simple uncluttered background, suitable for ages 6–14. Respectful, serene depiction of Indian mythology.

### Negative Prompt

> text, letters, watermark, logo, realistic gore, blood, violence, scary faces, dark horror mood, clutter, distorted hands, extra limbs, photo-realism, low resolution
